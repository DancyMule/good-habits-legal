# Delete your Good Habits account

Last updated: October 23, 2026

Good Habits, developed by DancyMule, lets you request the deletion of your account and its data.

## Delete your account in the app

1. Open Good Habits and sign in.
2. Tap **Profile** in the bottom bar.
3. Tap **Delete account** and confirm.

The deletion is immediate. An internet connection is required.

**If you subscribe to Premium, cancel the subscription before you delete your account.** Deleting your account does not cancel it. On your iPhone open Settings, tap your name, then Subscriptions. On Android open the Google Play Store, tap your profile picture, then Payments and subscriptions, then Subscriptions.

## Request deletion by email

If you can no longer use the app:

1. Send an email to [juarezjorge.ar@gmail.com](mailto:juarezjorge.ar@gmail.com) from the email address of the Google or Apple account you use in Good Habits. If you chose Hide My Email with Apple, write from the address that receives those emails.
2. Use the subject "Delete my Good Habits account".
3. We confirm the request by replying to that address and complete the deletion within 30 days.

## Delete some of your data

You can delete part of your data without deleting your account:

| Data | How to delete it |
|---|---|
| A habit or a habit group | Open it in the app and tap **Delete** |
| A routine you published | Open it in **Community** and tap **Unpublish** |
| Your community nickname | Change it in **Community** |
| Usage statistics and crash reports | Turn off **Share usage statistics** in **Profile** to stop sending them. What was already sent is not linked to your account and is deleted automatically (usage statistics after 14 months, crash reports after 90 days) |
| Anything else | Send an email to [juarezjorge.ar@gmail.com](mailto:juarezjorge.ar@gmail.com) from the email address of your Good Habits account with the subject "Delete my Good Habits data" and say what you want deleted. We reply to confirm and complete it within 30 days |

## What is deleted

| Data | Result |
|---|---|
| Profile (name, email, profile photo URL, Google and Apple identifiers) | Deleted |
| Sessions and sign-in credentials | Deleted |
| Habits and habit groups stored on our servers | Deleted |
| App preferences such as your mascot | Deleted |
| Plan information, free Premium records, and App Store and Google Play purchase records | Deleted |
| Community nickname, published routines, your reports, and hidden authors | Deleted. Copies other people already follow stay in their accounts |
| Server logs | Kept for up to 90 days for security, then deleted |
| Usage statistics and crash reports | Not linked to your account, so they cannot be found by it. They are deleted automatically: usage statistics after 14 months, crash reports after 90 days |
| Records we must keep by law | Kept only for the period the law requires |

## Data on your device

Habits and reminders also live on your phone. Uninstall the app, or on Android clear its data in settings, to remove them from the device.

If you signed in with Apple you can also stop using Sign in with Apple for Good Habits: on your iPhone open Settings, tap your name, then Sign in with Apple.
