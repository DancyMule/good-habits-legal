# Delete your Good Habits account

Last updated: September 25, 2026

Good Habits, developed by DancyMule, lets you request the deletion of your account and its data.

## How to request deletion

1. Send an email to [juarezjorge.ar@gmail.com](mailto:juarezjorge.ar@gmail.com) from the email address of the Google account you use in Good Habits.
2. Use the subject "Delete my Good Habits account".
3. We confirm the request by replying to that address and complete the deletion within 30 days.

## What is deleted

| Data | Result |
|---|---|
| Profile (name, email, profile photo URL, Google identifier) | Deleted |
| Sessions and sign-in credentials | Deleted |
| Habits stored on our servers | Deleted |
| Plan information | Deleted |
| Server logs | Kept for up to 90 days for security, then deleted |
| Records we must keep by law | Kept only for the period the law requires |

## Data on your device

Habits and reminders also live on your phone. Uninstall the app or clear its data in Android settings to remove them from the device.
