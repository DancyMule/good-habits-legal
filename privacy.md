# Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 25, 2026

This policy explains how Good Habits (the "app") handles your information. Good Habits is developed by DancyMule ("we" or "us"). If you have questions write to [juarezjorge.ar@gmail.com](mailto:juarezjorge.ar@gmail.com).

## Information we collect

| Category | What it includes | Where it is stored |
|---|---|---|
| Google account information | Name, email address, profile photo URL, and your Google account identifier. We receive it when you sign in with Google. | Our servers |
| Session credentials | Access and refresh tokens that keep you signed in. Our servers keep only a one-way hash of each token. | Encrypted on your device and hashed on our servers |
| Habit information | Habit name, optional description, schedule, color, and whether reminders are active. | On your device. When account synchronization is available the habit definition is also stored on our servers. |
| Reminder timing | The next time a reminder should fire. | Only on your device |
| Plan information | Your plan (for example free) and its limits. | Our servers |
| Technical data | IP address, request time, and error details our servers record while handling requests. | Our servers and hosting providers |

We do not collect your location, contacts, photos, or advertising identifier. The app does not include advertising or analytics SDKs.

## How we use information

- To create your account and sign you in.
- To store your habits and show local reminders on your device.
- To apply plan limits and keep the service secure.
- To respond to your requests and comply with legal obligations.

We process information to provide the service you requested, for our legitimate interest in keeping it secure and working, and when required by law. We do not sell your personal information and we do not use it for advertising.

## How information is shared

- **Google** verifies your identity when you sign in with Google. Google handles that data under its own privacy policy.
- **Service providers** host our servers and databases for us. They may process data only to provide that service.
- **Legal reasons.** We may disclose information when required by law or to protect the rights and safety of users and the service.

## Device backups

If Android backup is turned on for your device, Android may include your habits in your Google account backup. Your sign-in credentials are excluded from backups. You control this in your device settings.

## Retention

We keep your account information while your account exists. After a deletion request we delete your account data within 30 days. Server logs are kept for up to 90 days unless they are needed to investigate a security incident. Data on your device stays until you delete it or uninstall the app.

## Your choices and rights

Depending on where you live you may have the right to access, correct, delete, or export your information, and to object to or restrict some processing. To use any of these rights write to [juarezjorge.ar@gmail.com](mailto:juarezjorge.ar@gmail.com) from the email address linked to your account. We answer within 30 days. You may also complain to your local data protection authority.

You can turn off notifications at any time in your device settings.

## Account deletion

You can delete your account and its data from the app (About Good Habits, then Delete account) or ask us by email. See [Account deletion](../account-deletion/) for the steps.

## Security

We use encrypted connections, encrypt your session on the device, and store only hashes of session tokens on our servers. No method is perfectly secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

## Children

Good Habits is not directed to children under 13 or under the minimum age required in your country. We do not knowingly collect information from them. If you believe a child gave us information contact us and we will delete it.

## International transfers

Our servers and providers may be located in a country other than yours. When we transfer information we take reasonable steps so it stays protected as described here.

## Changes to this policy

We may update this policy. We will change the date at the top and, for significant changes, tell you in the app before they apply.
